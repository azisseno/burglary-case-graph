NARRATIVE
Responding officer was dispatched to a nail salon in Renton after a storefront was breached overnight. On arrival the officer found the glass front of the business broken out. Witness accounts and surveillance video indicate a black Jeep was backed through the storefront glass. The Jeep was stolen. The business owner and a witness gave accounts of what they saw and heard; those accounts were taken for comparison with the video. The attached camera stills are from the business's own camera system.

Point of entry: glass storefront.
Damage: glass front of the business broken out by the vehicle.
Items reported missing: not reported.

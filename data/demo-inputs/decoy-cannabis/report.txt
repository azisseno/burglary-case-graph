NARRATIVE
Responding officers were dispatched to a cannabis shop in Belltown, Seattle, after a stolen car was driven into the storefront. Officers believe five people originally smashed in. That group had left by the time officers arrived. Four men who entered the shop after the original group left were arrested and booked for commercial burglary. They were described as opportunists. The original group of five has not been identified. The stolen car was towed for evidence.

## 1. Long Beach "salesman" distraction burglaries (Long Beach, CA)

Sources
- Long Beach Police Department press release, 2024-04-01: https://longbeach.gov/police/press-releases/suspect-arrested-for-multiple-residential-burglaries--additional-victims-sought/

Series-level facts
- Same method in every incident, per police: "the suspect posed as a salesman and/or service worker and knocked on victim's door. When the victim(s) did not appear to be home, the suspect entered the victim's home" (LBPD).

- Repeat blocks: 5200 block Pageantry St on 2023-11-10 and 2023-11-20; 5300 block East 29th St on 2024-02-22 and 2024-03-22.

## 3. Pico-Union rear-window crew (Los Angeles, CA)

Source
- LAPD newsroom release NR23717am, 2023-10-19, updated 2023-10-20: https://www.lapdonline.org/newsroom/residential-burglary-suspects-arrested-in-pico-union-nr23717am/

Incident summary (the release does not itemize dates)

| Date | Time | Location | Scene | Entry | Items | Evidence mentioned |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| not reported (5 burglaries recognized as a series on 2023-10-04) | not reported | Pico-Union, Los Angeles (Rampart Division) | residences | four masked suspects "prying rear windows and doors" | cash and jewelry; about $100,000 across the incidents | surveillance footage from the burglaries; one victim video of suspects inside; a second location showed the suspect vehicle leaving |

How investigators linked the incidents
- Rampart burglary detectives "realized there were five residential burglaries" on 2023-10-04 (LAPD).
- Footage showed four masked suspects with the same pry method and the same getaway vehicle, described as a white 2024 Honda HR-V (plate given in the release; not recorded here).
- Officers surveilled a possible vehicle, saw "an additional vehicle with an exact match," made a traffic stop, and detained three men. LAPD says they matched the earlier burglaries "including tattoo's, clothing, and physical description."
- The majority of the victims were of Asian descent (LAPD).

Outcome
- Three arrested; district attorney filed burglary charges on two of the three (LAPD, 2023-10-19). Police believe the crew is responsible for about 30 residential burglaries in the Rampart (Pico-Union) and Hollenbeck areas. Later outcome not reported.

Data gaps

## 5. Athlete-home crews and a New York City pawn-shop fence (multi-state)

Sources
- CNN, 2025-02-05: https://www.cnn.com/2025/02/05/us/men-arrested-newyork-burglary-nfl-star
- ABC News, 2025-02-04: https://abcnews.go.com/US/men-accused-buying-selling-luxury-items-linked-burglaries/story?id=118457516
- CBS New York (guilty plea; article undated in the text read, plea stated as the year after the Dec 2024 burglary): https://www.cbsnews.com/newyork/news/joe-burrow-burglary-nyc-pawn-shop-owner-guilty/

Incident table (victims are professional athletes; names left out)

| Date | Time | Location | Scene | Entry | Items | Evidence mentioned |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Oct 2024 | not reported | home of a Kansas City Chiefs player | residence | not reported | not reported | not reported (CNN) |
| Oct 2024 | not reported | home of a second Kansas City Chiefs player | residence | not reported | not reported | not reported (CNN) |
| 2024-12-09 | not reported | Anderson Township, OH | residence, owner away at a game | "shattered bedroom window," home "ransacked" (CBS) | jewelry, watches, designer luggage and glasses appear in photos the crew took (CBS); loss total not reported | later recovery of a shirt and cap believed stolen (ABC, CBS) |
| late 2024 | not reported | homes of NBA players | residences | not reported | not reported | listed as targets (CNN) |

- CNN also reports a separate Orange County, CA district attorney video of thieves entering a home through an upper bedroom window. CNN does not say this belongs to the same crew, so treat it as related coverage, not a linked incident.
- FBI bulletin of 2024-12-20: homes are targeted because "they may have high-end goods like designer handbags, jewelry, watches, and cash" (CNN). Crews hit homes while athletes are playing in other cities (CBS).

How investigators linked the incidents and the goods
- On 2025-01-10 an Ohio State Highway Patrol agent saw suspicious luggage carried into a vehicle outside a hotel in Fairborn, OH. Inside were a shirt and cap "believed to be stolen from the December 9, 2024 burglary" (ABC, quoting the affidavit). Four Chilean nationals were arrested (ABC, CNN).
- Police found photos the burglars took of themselves with the spoils (CBS).
- Resale chain: phone records and video surveillance linked a Manhattan pawn-shop operator to at least two of the four crew members. A crew member contacted him less than a week before the Ohio burglary (ABC).
- Federal raid on 2025-02-04 at the pawn shop and New Jersey storage units; more than 100 high-end watches plus jewelry and handbags seized; prosecutors estimated up to $5 million in stolen property (CNN). Undercover detective made seven controlled sales of purported stolen goods between Oct 2022 and Jan 2024 (ABC, CNN).
- Indictment covers receipts of stolen property from Nov 2020 onward; evidence also tied the shop to one "prolific burglar" who hit at least 16 homes across the US in 2019 and 2020 (CNN).

Outcome
- Four crew members arrested Jan 2025. Two fences indicted 2025-02-04 and pleaded not guilty 2025-02-05. One pleaded guilty to conspiracy to receive stolen property (maximum five years, restitution about $2.5 million); the second pleaded guilty a month earlier with sentencing set for December (CBS). Year of the pleas is stated only as the year after the Dec 2024 burglary. Sentences not reported. The investigation "led to at least six arrests" (CBS).

Data gaps
- Per-incident times, entry methods outside Ohio, loss values, and which seized items match which burglary are not reported. The fence was not charged in connection with a specific burglary (CBS).
- Victims are public figures; this file leaves their names out per the brief.

- NBC Los Angeles, 2023-12-14: https://www.nbclosangeles.com/news/local/oc-sheriffs-crack-down-on-dangerous-prolific-crew-that-stole-range-rover-guns-and-jewelry-from-homes/3291709/

Incident summary (no per-incident list published in these sources)

| Date | Time | Location | Scene | Entry | Items | Evidence mentioned |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Sep 2023 (start of case): 3 burglaries | not reported | 2 in unincorporated Santa Ana, 1 in Yorba Linda (ABC7) | residences | not reported | not reported | not reported |
| through Dec 2023: 34 burglaries total | not reported | 19 in Fullerton, remainder handled by sheriff's deputies (ABC7); 3 occurred with residents home (NBC) | residences | often through a second-floor balcony or awning and the master bedroom (NBC, ABC7) | cash, weapons, jewelry, valuables, one Range Rover; about $500,000 total (ABC7, NBC) | surveillance cameras and second-floor motion sensors cited (NBC) |

How investigators linked the incidents
- A task team grew from the three September residential burglaries into a 34-burglary case (ABC7). Nineteen police departments helped (NBC).
- Asked how suspects were linked, the sheriff "couldn't share specifics" but pointed to surveillance cameras and second-floor motion sensors (NBC).
- Described tactics: monitoring social media for who is out of town, surveilling pattern of life, and entering via second floor to avoid motion detectors (ABC7).

Outcome
- Nine arrested in a sweep on 2023-12-13; 11 still sought; 20 charged, including five juveniles (ABC7). Seven guns and $40,000 in cash, jewelry and handbags recovered in the sweep (ABC7). Case disposition not reported.
- NBC says the ring operated "for more than a year" while ABC7 says the case began in September; the sources differ.

Data gaps
- No incident dates, blocks or per-incident items. Link evidence withheld.

Time of day is "not reported" in every incident table below unless a cell says otherwise.

Imagery rule used in this file: "verified" means I opened the government page or downloaded its images and looked at them. "Unverified" means the page was blocked to me or only a news outlet showed the image with a government credit. Image URLs are given exactly as they appear on the government page.

## 1. Long Beach "salesman" distraction burglaries (Long Beach, CA)

Sources
- Long Beach Police Department press release, 2024-04-01: https://longbeach.gov/police/press-releases/suspect-arrested-for-multiple-residential-burglaries--additional-victims-sought/
- City News Service via MyNewsLA, 2024-04-04 (district attorney filing and arraignment): https://mynewsla.com/crime/2024/04/04/man-pleads-not-guilty-in-series-of-residential-burglaries-in-long-beach/

Series-level facts
- Same method in every incident, per police: "the suspect posed as a salesman and/or service worker and knocked on victim's door. When the victim(s) did not appear to be home, the suspect entered the victim's home" (LBPD).
- Items taken across the series: jewelry, collectible coins, heirlooms, jewelry boxes, money (LBPD). Per-incident items are not reported.
- Area: "East Division" of Long Beach (LBPD). Related incidents also in Glendale, Los Alamitos, Cerritos and Torrance (LBPD); those are not itemized.

How investigators linked the incidents and the goods
- On 2025-01-10 an Ohio State Highway Patrol agent saw suspicious luggage carried into a vehicle outside a hotel in Fairborn, OH. Inside were a shirt and cap "believed to be stolen from the December 9, 2024 burglary" (ABC, quoting the affidavit). Four Chilean nationals were arrested (ABC, CNN).
- Police found photos the burglars took of themselves with the spoils (CBS).

Undercover detective made seven controlled sales of purported stolen goods between Oct 2022 and Jan 2024 (ABC, CNN).
- Indictment covers receipts of stolen property from Nov 2020 onward; evidence also tied the shop to one "prolific burglar" who hit at least 16 homes across the US in 2019 and 2020 (CNN).

Outcome
- Four crew members arrested Jan 2025. Two fences indicted 2025-02-04 and pleaded not guilty 2025-02-05. One pleaded guilty to conspiracy to receive stolen property (maximum five years, restitution about $2.5 million); the second pleaded guilty a month earlier with sentencing set for December (CBS). Year of the pleas is stated only as the year after the Dec 2024 burglary. Sentences not reported. The investigation "led to at least six arrests" (CBS).

Update on outcome (not verified by opening the page): a search result summarizing the Eastern District of New York release https://www.justice.gov/usao-edny/pr/diamond-district-fence-sentenced-46-months-connection-large-scale-transnational-stolen says one fence was sentenced to 46 months. Related releases: https://www.justice.gov/usao-edny/pr/two-individuals-charged-running-fencing-operation-south-american-theft-groups (charges) and https://www.justice.gov/usao-edny/pr/diamond-district-fence-pleads-guilty-connection-large-scale-stolen-property-operation (plea).
